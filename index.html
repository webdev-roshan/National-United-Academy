About National United School

Established in 2000 AD (2057 B.S.) in Budhanilkantha, Kathmandu, National United School (नेशनल युनाइटेड स्कुल) teaches with the motto "तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय" (Lead Us From Darkness Unto Light). Morning assembly, classroom learning, and life beyond the timetable sit side by side on one campus.

Campus Life

A Disciplined Assembly Ground

Students gather each morning in the school courtyard with the national flag and house colours. The campus includes classrooms, a cafeteria, a sports house, and space for the whole school to stand together.
